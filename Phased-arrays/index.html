The model rebuilds from your hardware settings: element count, spacing, frequency (sets physical size), substrate and semiconductor choice. Large arrays show a 16 × 16 element tile, which is how real AESAs are built and replaced.

3D radiation pattern

drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · radius and colour = gain (dB, 50 dB range)

The aperture sits in the XY plane with boresight along +Z. Azimuth steering tilts the lobe in the XZ plane. Elements are coloured by commanded phase, the plain arrow is the steer command, the red arrow is the jammer direction. The 3D lobe uses the separable product AFx(u)·AFy(v)·cosqθ with u = sinθ cosφ, v = sinθ sinφ, so it includes taper, quantisation and nulling on the azimuth axis.
