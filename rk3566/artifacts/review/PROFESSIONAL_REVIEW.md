# Independent professional review of the completed simulation output

**Full-board validation readiness: 2.5/10.** The completed work is a useful, traceable study of simplified power behavior and selected local phenomena. It does not yet establish that the manufactured RK3566 board will start reliably, meet every component's operating limits, remain thermally acceptable, or satisfy EMI/EMC requirements.

**Presentation: 8/10. Numerical implementation within the declared models: 8/10.** Those judgments assess the web application, video and mathematical work separately. They contribute no extra points to the full-board readiness score. Attractive plots, a real SPICE solver, and many passing software tests do not establish that the inputs and physical models represent the assembled board.

This review covers the completed artifacts for PCB SHA-256 `3ba4da7036173e31941ea22b041d1a2a3433c2bcdedccf1a75b1f52b31ce2807`, reviewed on 2026-09-14. Paths are relative to `rk3566-sbc-rev4/`. The score is an engineering review judgment, not a probability of success, certification or measured reliability figure. No hardware was changed for this review.

## What the completed work does well

The native source is traceable: 422 schematic/BOM components, 285 nets and 1,662 endpoints pass schematic/PCB endpoint parity. Unsupported quantities remain unknown rather than being replaced with fabricated per-component currents. The audit distinguishes 405 populated references from 17 DNP references. It reports missing explicit MPN metadata honestly; some identities remain in values/notes and testpoints do not need procurement numbers, so that metadata count is not a direct count of unidentified physical parts. Evidence: `simulation/data/board.json`, `simulation/data/electrical-audit.json`.

The local mathematical work has useful independent checks. The U7 transient dataset retains runnable netlists, raw samples, KCL/charge/energy checks and timestep comparisons. The electric solver has analytical plate/dielectric checks plus mesh/domain sensitivity. The magnetic kernel is checked against independent formulas/integration and explicitly labels invented component bridges and returns. The thermal illustration is checked against numerical integration and does not assign temperatures to other components. Evidence: `simulation/data/time-domain.json`, `simulation/data/electric-field.json`, `simulation/data/video/thermal-data.json`, and their test modules.

The retained test log reports **75 passing software tests**. Browser and video checks cover playback/seeking/downloads, source identity, units, unknown values and separate physical clocks. The 74-second H.264 video decodes successfully and visibly labels the thermal chapter as L219-only and illustrative. I reviewed the saved electric-section screenshot and decoded thermal frame. These support the presentation score. The smaller model-scope annotations could be easier to read, and an overall readiness indicator should be more prominent than a nominal model-check score. Evidence: `simulation/data/test-results.txt`, `simulation/data/time-domain-browser-validation.json`, `simulation/data/video/video-validation.json`.

The numerical score is limited to the implemented equations and retained checks. The present tests do not independently validate the entire physical model, field boundaries, real controller or fabricated board. Passing conservation laws is necessary for a solver; it does not prove that all relevant losses, sources and constraints are included.

## The gaps that determine the readiness score

1. **The main rail model uses assumed loads and averaged ramps.** A software-derived 2.70 A startup peak or 20.85 ms rail-ready time is a scenario result. It is not a verified RK3566 current waveform or actual RK809-5 power sequence. The nominal checker uses generic ±10% targets on some rails; those do not replace SoC DVFS operating limits. Evidence: `simulation/data/latest-result.json`, `simulation/engine.py`.
2. **U7's detailed switching model still prescribes its switch-node voltage.** It does not contain TPS566242 control, compensation, current limiting, soft start or real input commutation. The 20.22 A cold experiment exceeds the constant-inductor model's useful range and is correctly marked illustrative. A programmed duty ramp is a mathematical comparison, not the actual control law. Evidence: `simulation/data/time-domain/*.cir`, `simulation/TIME_DOMAIN_EM_ANALYSIS.md`.
3. **Important power components still require qualification.** L201–L205 are generic 470 nH inductors; L206 is a 240 nH CPU inductor without a qualified part/application model. R227's exact power-rated shunt selection remains open. Wi-Fi VBAT has a narrow documented voltage margin through FB202. RK809 switch ratings/variant data require resolution. These are concrete design inputs to close, not issues that can be cured by a better-looking plot. Evidence: findings `cpu-inductor`, `pmic-inductors`, `cpu-shunt`, `wifi-headroom`, `source-switch-conflict` in `simulation/data/electrical-audit.json`.
4. **The corner campaign contains four retained failures, with different meanings.** Slow startup and deliberately early reset expose sequencing sensitivities; brownout is an injected disturbance. They do not automatically prove those conditions occur on the board. The combined low-input/high-load/reduced-capacitance case exposes a regulation concern under its assumed simultaneous conditions. Establish whether each condition is required, then test the applicable physical design. Do not delete failed runs or lower the assumed loads to manufacture an 8/10 result. Evidence: runs 0011, 0012, 0014 and 0016 in `simulation/data/runs/`.
5. **Native connectivity and fields cover different questions.** DRC retains 61 differential-gap findings, eight uncoupled-length findings, two skew findings and 176 co-located-hole findings. These are not 247 measured electrical failures; they require justified resolution or documented exceptions. One grounded coplanar electric section and prescribed output-loop B/H fields do not solve the board's return distribution, DDR timing, input hot loop, inductor core leakage, harness radiation or immunity. Evidence: `simulation/data/native-drc.json`, `simulation/data/em-cross-section-geometry.json`, `simulation/TIME_DOMAIN_EM_ANALYSIS.md`.
6. **Temperature and boot remain major missing domains.** A 26.406 °C illustrative L219 node at 60 seconds uses assumed thermal resistance/capacitance and only held DCR loss. It gives no RK3566/PMIC temperature or PCB heat-spreading result. Proposed rail/reset timing gives no DDR-training or operating-system boot evidence. Evidence: `simulation/VIDEO_THERMAL_METHOD.md`, boot findings in `simulation/data/electrical-audit.json`.

## Professional workflow and evidence gates

Choose tools by the physical question and available models. AI can assist extraction, netlist preparation, source review, scripting and checking. Its interpretation should be independently reviewable. The solver/model identity, assumptions, input decks, raw results and applicability checks remain the technical evidence.

| Work package | Tool capability required | Evidence gate before claiming completion |
|---|---|---|
| Actual regulator transients | A circuit simulator compatible with the exact manufacturer's control/protection model | Reproduce the vendor reference circuit; verify part variant/pin mapping; include actual L/C/ESR/bias and input paths; test startup, load steps, dropout, protection and stability over required operating corners |
| Whole power tree | Circuit-level models or validated system-level abstractions connected to source-qualified load profiles | Every rail has applicable limits, credible startup/steady/transient demand and sequencing; external J120/J121 sources are modeled; back-power paths, Kelvin drops and thermal dissipation are assessed |
| SI/PI and local/full EM | Appropriate parasitic extraction, transmission-line/channel and field solving tools with validated ports/boundaries | Qualified stack/material/package data; resolved differential/reference exceptions; DDR/USB/CSI channel checks; PDN/return models; mesh/boundary convergence and correlation for the actual product geometry |
| Thermal | Applicable loss models plus mounted component/PCB thermal networks or distributed heat-transfer model | Realistic IC/core/AC/copper losses, material/contact/airflow inputs and temperature-dependent behavior; measured temperature/transient correlation at stated probe locations |
| Boot | Board-specific firmware/configuration and physical hardware instrumentation | Actual RK809-5 OTP/configuration, clock/reset waveforms, qualified DDR initialization, repeated cold/warm/brownout boots and retained logs |
| EMC/bench | Calibrated measurements and applicable test setups | Identified product configuration/limits, supply/load/thermal correlation, appropriate conducted/radiated/immunity evidence, uncertainty and discrepancy closure |

LTspice, ngspice or another circuit simulator should be selected after checking model compatibility and what the manufacturer actually models. Circuit simulation is one work package in this plan. It does not replace boot execution, distributed EM, mounted-board thermal inputs or laboratory correlation. Any newly located model or software capability should first be assessed against the gates above; finding it is not evidence that this board has passed.

Use **8/10 plus mandatory gates**, not a score alone, for a future engineering-readiness milestone. Mandatory gates are: no unresolved critical operating-limit violation; exact power/boot configuration established; applicable high-speed/return/thermal analyses complete; repeatable real boot with rail correlation; and EMC evidence appropriate to the intended release claim. Keep an unresolved gate visibly open even if other categories earn enough points. Any required certification or product release approval remains separate.

The next useful step is to replace the highest-impact assumptions with applicable physical models and measurements, starting with the actual power converters and PMIC configuration. The existing application can remain the results viewer. Keep every historical dataset and update the engineering conclusions only when new evidence supports them. The detailed pending work is already separated in `simulation/NEXT_AGENT_HANDOFF.md` and EM-02 onward in `simulation/TIME_DOMAIN_EM_ANALYSIS.md`.
