My relay won’t open. What should I check?
  1. Tailscale must say Connected on this device and on the relay laptop.
  2. Both devices must be on the same Tailscale network. Look for the relay laptop in the Tailscale device list.
  3. Keep the relay laptop awake and the Agent Remote launcher running.
  4. On Android, check that your browser or Agent Remote app is included in Tailscale’s app routing. Another VPN may replace the Tailscale connection.
  5. Use Test private connection above. If the new tab shows "ok":true, the network route works. Return to the dashboard for login.

This public page can’t inspect your VPN or private relay in the background. The connection test opens directly on your private relay.

Do I need an APK or a paid server?

A browser works on phones, tablets, and laptops; the existing Android app works too. The relay runs on your own laptop, so server rental is $0. Tailscale’s free Personal plan is for eligible personal, non-commercial use. Model-provider subscriptions/API usage, internet access, and laptop electricity remain separate.
